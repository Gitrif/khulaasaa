1. Demised Property Description & Permitted Use

The Landlord agrees to let and the Tenant agrees to lease the residential apartment premises located at: Lot 10423, Nirolhu Magu, Hulhumale Phase 1, strictly for private residential domicile only. Commercial operations or unauthorized occupancy are prohibited.

2. Term of Tenancy

The tenancy shall commence on the execution date for a fixed period of 12 consecutive calendar months. Either party may request a renewal by serving a written notice not less than 30 days prior to lease expiry.

3. Monthly Rent, Due Date & Advance Payment

The agreed monthly rent shall be MVR 22,000.00, payable on or before the 5th day of each month into the Landlord's designated bank account.

4. Late Payment Daily Fine

Should the Tenant fail to settle the full monthly rent by the agreed due date, a daily late fee of MVR 100.00 per calendar day of delay shall accrue until the total overdue rent is settled.

5. Security Deposit, Deductions & Excess Liability

The Tenant has deposited with the Landlord a Security Deposit of MVR 44,000.00. This deposit shall be refundable within fourteen (14) calendar days following final inspection and key handover upon vacating. The Landlord is explicitly authorized to deduct from this deposit: (a) any outstanding utility bills (STELCO electricity, MWSC water, WAMCO waste), and (b) verified costs required to clean, paint, or restore the demised premises and inventory to the handover condition or better (fair wear and tear excepted). Should the Security Deposit prove insufficient to satisfy the total unpaid utilities, cleaning, and repair damages, the Tenant remains legally and personally liable for the remaining deficit balance, which shall constitute an enforceable debt owed to the Landlord payable within seven (7) calendar days of demand.

6. Utilities, Garbage & Municipal Upkeep

The Tenant shall bear and punctually pay all monthly electricity, water consumption, and WAMCO municipal garbage management fees. Proof of settled utility bills must be presented upon vacating.

7. Public Area Maintenance & Tenant Interior Upkeep

(a) Lessor Common Area Upkeep: The Landlord covenants to maintain all public and common areas in clean, safe, and fully functional condition, including: stairwell, corridor, and entrance lighting; scheduled elevator/lift servicing and safety inspections; regular cleaning of common stairs, landings, and entrance lobbies; and maintenance of the central building water booster pump system.

(b) Tenant Interior Upkeep: The Tenant covenants to keep the interior of the apartment clean, sanitary, and well-maintained throughout the lease, and to deliver it upon termination in the exact same clean and good state as received, or better. The cost to clean and restore the premises to this standard shall be deducted from the security deposit, and if insufficient, the Tenant shall owe and pay the difference. The Tenant shall promptly replace interior consumables (light bulbs, filters) and repair any damages arising from tenant negligence.

8. Prohibition of Illegal Activities & Anti-Subletting

The Tenant covenants not to commit, permit, or cause any act contrary to the Laws and Islamic Shari'ah of the Republic of Maldives. Subletting, assignment, or granting lodging to third parties without prior written consent of the Landlord is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate termination.

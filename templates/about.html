About Khulaasaa

Khulaasaa is an independent, non-partisan Maldivian news wire, public procurement monitor, and employment intelligence platform designed to deliver verified, multi-outlet synthesis of developing national events.

1. Purpose & Editorial Mission

In the fast-paced Maldivian news cycle, breaking stories are frequently covered across different independent newsrooms with varying angles, timestamps, and details. Readers often find themselves switching between multiple portals to understand the complete picture.

Khulaasaa solves this by providing a unified, neutral index that cross-references stories across all accredited Maldivian publishers, creating a transparent, chronological summary that directs all reading traffic straight back to the original reporting newsrooms.

2. Core Platform Pillars

Multi-Outlet News Cross-Check

Compares live reporting across independent outlets (Mihaaru, Sun Online, Dhauru, Atoll Times, PSM News, Avas, The Edition, and Haftha), allowing citizens to compare perspectives in real time.

Chronological Narrative Timelines

Follows developing national policies, legal proceedings, major infrastructure developments, and crisis situations from day one through verified timelines.

Government Gazette & Tenders

Indexes public procurement tenders, requests for proposals (RFPs), and state enterprise contracts from gazette.gov.mv to foster open economic participation.

Maldivian Careers & CV Builder

Provides access to civil service and private sector job opportunities with our built-in 4-style Maldivian CV creator and instant Telegram & Viber vacancy alerts.

3. Editorial Standards & Attribution Guarantee

  • Strict Neutrality: We do not publish op-eds, partisan commentary, or unverified claims. Summaries reflect purely factual multi-outlet consensus.
  • Zero Full-Text Scraping: Khulaasaa strictly does not republish full-text journalism. We only index headlines, timestamps, and factual summaries.
  • 100% Traffic Referral: Every article links directly to the original publisher, sending readers, pageviews, and ad revenue directly to independent journalists.

REPUBLIC OF MALDIVES

RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT

Standard Maldivian Lease Framework · Act No. 23/2010 Customary Lease Compliance

THIS RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT is made and entered into on this 25 October 2026, by and between:

THE LANDLORD: Hussain Solih (NID: A012345), residing permanently at M. Alivaage, Male' (Phone: 7789123).
THE TENANT: Ibrahim Mohamed (NID: A129845), residing permanently at H. Vinares, Male' (Phone: 7994422).

1. Demised Property Description

The Landlord agrees to let and the Tenant agrees to take the residential apartment premises located at: Lot 10423, Nirolhu Magu, Hulhumale Phase 1, strictly for private residential use only.

2. Term of Lease

The duration of this tenancy shall be for a fixed term of 12 consecutive calendar months commencing on the date hereof, renewable upon mutual written agreement by both parties giving not less than thirty (30) days notice prior to expiration.

3. Monthly Rent & Security Deposit

The agreed monthly rent shall be MVR 22,000.00, payable in advance on or before the 5th day of each month into the Landlord's designated bank account.

The Tenant has paid to the Landlord a Security Deposit of MVR 44,000.00. This deposit shall be refundable within 14 calendar days after handover upon termination, subject to deductions for unpaid utilities or tenant-inflicted damage beyond fair wear and tear.

4. Utilities, Upkeep & Prohibitions

The Tenant covenants to promptly pay all electricity (STELCO), water (MWSC), and municipal waste charges. The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or part with possession of the premises to any third party without express written permission of the Landlord.

Signed by Landlord:
Hussain Solih
Landlord / Owner

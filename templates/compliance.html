ކ Legal Compliance & Publisher Takedown Desk

Khulaasaa operates under strict compliance with the Copyright and Related Rights Act of the Republic of Maldives, maintaining 100% canonical attribution and an expedited publisher opt-out procedure.

1. Maldivian Legal Basis & Fair Dealing Framework

Khulaasaa operates in full alignment with the laws of the Republic of Maldives, specifically the Copyright and Related Rights Act (Act No. 23/2010) and international standards for search engines, news indices, and informational aggregators:

Section 24: Fair Dealing for Reporting of Current Events

Under Section 24 of Act No. 23/2010, the reproduction of short excerpts, headlines, and factual summaries for the purpose of reporting current events, commentary, and public information is lawful fair dealing, provided accurate source attribution is maintained.

Zero Full-Text Republishing Policy

Khulaasaa guarantees that it never reproduces, mirrors, or scrapes full-text articles or bypasses digital paywalls. Only publicly accessible headlines, timestamps, short factual excerpts, and publisher branding are indexed.

Direct Canonical Traffic Redirection

Every indexed story contains prominent direct hyperlinks to the original reporting newsroom. All reading traffic and user visits are channeled directly to the original publisher's website.

2. Public Sector Gazette & RTI Transparency

Tender announcements and procurement notifications are aggregated directly from the official Government Gazette of the Republic of Maldives (gazette.gov.mv) and verified public SOE procurement portals in accordance with the Right to Information Act (Act No. 1/2014) to enhance competitive procurement transparency and fair economic participation.

3. Trademarks & Publisher Property

All registered trademarks, publication logos, mastheads, and article titles referenced on Khulaasaa remain the exclusive intellectual property of their respective owners and news agencies (including Mihaaru, Sun Online, Dhauru, Atoll Times, PSM News, Avas, The Edition, and Haftha). Khulaasaa uses these identifiers solely for nominative identification and newsroom attribution.

4. Content Removal & Publisher Opt-Out Procedure

Fast-Track SLA

Khulaasaa respects the editorial autonomy and intellectual property rights of all media organizations, journalists, and individuals. If you are a copyright owner, publisher, or subject of an indexed notice wishing to request a correction, suppression, or complete removal:

How to Submit a Removal or Correction Request:

Please email our compliance desk with the following details:

  1. The specific URL(s) on Khulaasaa where the indexed summary appears.
  2. The source URL(s) on the publisher website or legal justification.
  3. Your name, organization, and authorization or relationship to the content.
  4. Requested action: (a) Correction, (b) URL link update, (c) Full removal/opt-out.
